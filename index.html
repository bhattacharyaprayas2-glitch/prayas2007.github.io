About & Engineering Philosophy

First-principles hardware intuition bridged with embedded telemetry and modern software tooling.

Pursuing B.Sc. Electronics (Honours) with Mathematics, Computer Science, and Physics at Asutosh College (University of Calcutta), I build circuits from the silicon and transistor junction upward.

I prioritize discrete BJT switching (BC547, BC548, BD139, BD140, SL100), operational amplifiers (LM358, UA741), and dead-bug/veroboard prototyping over defaulting to microcontrollers—integrating Arduino and Python when active signal acquisition is needed.
